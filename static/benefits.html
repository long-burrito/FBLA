Perks of Joining Our Team

More than just a profession, working at the donut store is a joyful experience that involves more than just creating mouthwatering donuts. Every smile is as vital to our little shop as every sprinkle on our delicious desserts. Upon joining our team, you will become a member of a close-knit community that values creativity, collaboration, and the satisfaction that comes from brightening someone's day, one donut at a time.

The Numbers

In addition to brightening customers' days, we offer a very competitive $18/hr wage. Our commitment to your growth goes beyond the kitchen - we offer tuition assistance, providing you with the support needed to pursue your educational goals when you work with us for at least 6 months.
